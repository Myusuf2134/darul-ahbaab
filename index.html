About us

Rooted in the Sunnah, built for our community.

Darul Ahbaab is committed to sharing the Prophetic Sunnah of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ — through both study and the living example of his revered way of life.

We recognize the obligation every believer carries to seek Islamic knowledge. With a nurturing, inclusive community and a sincere passion for Islam, we work together — with the help of Allah — to support the ummah in fulfilling that trust.

What we teach

Five disciplines, one way of life.

Each program is guided by knowledgeable, experienced scholars — building understanding step by step, from recitation to refinement of the heart.

01

Quran

القرآن

Study and memorize the Quran under the guidance of knowledgeable and experienced scholars.

02

Hadith

الحديث

Learn and practice the Sunnah of our beloved Prophet Muhammad ﷺ — his words, actions, and guidance.

03

Arabic

العربية

Explore Arabic grammar and the related disciplines that open the door to the Quran and its sciences.

04

Fiqh

الفقه

Learn the proper methods of prayer and fasting, and how to practice Islam by the Quran and Sunnah.

05

Suluk السلوك

Purify the heart by distancing yourself from what displeases Allah, and draw closer to Him by refining both your character and your heart.
